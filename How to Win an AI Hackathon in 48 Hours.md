## How to Win an AI Hackathon in 48 Hours

**Using Python + JavaScript + AI — a battle-tested playbook for speed, impact, and demo-day domination.**

Join **Andriy Kostenko**, a Full-Stack Software Engineer, for a practical session on how to design, build, and present a winning AI project in just **48 hours**.

In this talk, Andriy shares the exact strategy he used to win an AI hackathon — from choosing the right idea and tech stack to building a fast MVP and delivering a demo judges remember.

Rather than focusing on perfect architecture, this session reveals how to **prioritize speed, clarity, and impact**, using modern AI tools and a streamlined stack.
